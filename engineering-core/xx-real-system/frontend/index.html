REFERENCE ARCHITECTURE

One request, several kinds of truth

Synchronous orchestration decides whether to accept and route a request. PostgreSQL commits payment and outbox truth atomically. Redis makes asynchronous work visible. Independent ledger, settlement, and reconciliation stages protect financial correctness.

Realistic concepts

Explicit actors, authorization holds, state machine, double-entry journals, routing, risk, unknown outcomes, outbox, idempotency, settlement and reconciliation.

Simplified simulations

Providers, banks, network messages, clearing files, webhook endpoints, fees, disputes and bank balances are deterministic local abstractions.

Intentionally absent

No real PANs, bank credentials, PCI scope, HSM, live rails, multi-region consensus, regulatory reporting, or claim of proprietary implementation.
